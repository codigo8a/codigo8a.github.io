## Title

TankStrike — a turn-based tank duel in your browser

## Short description

Program six moves a round, outlast two rival tanks, and be the last one standing.

## Description

TankStrike is a turn-based tactics duel on a 12x12 arena. You command one tank
against two AI opponents. Every round you queue up to six commands — move,
turn, fire a laser, detonate a blast, or burn turbo — and all three tanks
resolve them at the same time, one step at a time.

Five hit points, three lives. Lose a life and you respawn with full health;
lose them all and you are out for good. The last tank with lives wins.

Every round is a small puzzle: turn to line up the shot, close the distance,
push a rival toward the edge, or spend a precious blast charge to clear the
tanks beside you. The bots stalk, line up their own lasers, and get more
aggressive when they have been quiet for a round or two.

No install, no account, and no server. The whole game runs in your browser.
